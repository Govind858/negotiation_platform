AI-Powered Manufacturing Procurement

Procurement Negotiation Intelligence & Institutional Memory

Empowering buyers to win raw material negotiations with automated pre-call strategy, zero-cost historical deal benchmarks, and structured feedback loops.

01. The Problem

Why Manufacturing Companies Overpay for Materials

1

Unprepared Buyers

Buyers enter supplier phone calls without knowing their true leverage, hidden weak points, or walkaway boundaries, leading to quick concessions.

2

Institutional Amnesia

When an experienced procurement manager leaves, years of supplier pricing tactics, concessions, and winning strategies disappear from the company.

3

The New Employee Trap

New staff buyers are unaware of historical prices. Suppliers easily charge them 15–20% higher rates for materials previously bought much cheaper.

02. The Solution

An Intelligent Negotiation CRM for Procurement

🧠

Pre-Call AI Battlecards

A 2-minute questionnaire analyzes price gaps, commodity dynamics, and urgency to produce model counter-replies and concession playbooks.

⚡

Instant Deal Matching

Automatically detects if the company bought this item before. Gives new staff the historical golden price and winning advice on day one.

🔄

Closed-Loop Memory Bank

Every Won deal records cost savings and winning tactics; every Lost deal records vendor floor prices and walkaway discipline.

03. Domain Engine

The 3 Core Flows That Power the System

1

Intake & Pre-Call AI Battlecard

5-stage questionnaire capturing specs, scale, urgency, and walkaway boundaries. Gemini 2.0 Flash calculates leverage score (0-100) and predicts supplier objections.

2

The Historical Deal Matching Engine

PostgreSQL Trigram (pg_trgm) runs natively in <5ms. Matches material names and grades to surface past winning benchmarks without third-party fees.

3

Multi-Round Logging & Deal Closeout Feedback

Tracks round-by-round commercial counter-offers (Price, Lead time, Terms, MOQ, Notes). Calculates verified financial savings on Won deals and logs vendor risk on Lost deals.

06. Database Design

Just 4 Tables for the Entire Platform

1. organizations

Multi-tenant isolation for each manufacturing company.

2. users

Staff accounts with 4 RBAC roles: Admin, Manager, Negotiator, Viewer.

3. invitations

Tokenized invite links for frictionless, zero-password staff onboarding.

4. negotiations (Master Table)

Stores intake answers, evolving rounds array, AI strategy, and Won/Lost outcomes in a single living record.

💡 Key Architecture Choice: No messy joins across 10 tables. The negotiation document evolves from intake $\to$ rounds $\to$ closure inside one indexed record.
