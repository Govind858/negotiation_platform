### Slide 1: Stages 1, 2 & 3 (Specs, Scale & Commercial Boundaries)

* **Slide Title:** The Intake Questionnaire: Specs, Volume & Financials (Stages 1–3)
* **Subtitle:** 12 Essential Parameters Defining Material Constraints and Purchasing Power
* **Layout:** 3 Horizontal Cards (Specs, Scale, Financials)

#### Slide Content:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📦 STAGE 1: ITEM & TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS                                             │
│ • 1.1 Item Name: Primary search key for matching past company purchases (e.g., SS304). │
│ • 1.2 Grade / Spec: Strict technical matching; stops comparing high-grade with low-grade.│
│ • 1.3 Customization Level: Commodity vs. Custom-Made. Detects supplier lock-in & power.│
│ • 1.4 Quality Criticality: Commercial vs. Zero-Defect. Prepares quality penalty clauses.│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 📊 STAGE 2: SCALE & ORDER VOLUME LEVERAGE                                              │
│ • 2.1 Order Quantity & Unit: Establishes baseline purchasing scale (e.g., 5,000 kg).   │
│ • 2.2 Purchase Frequency: Spot vs. Annual Contract. Unlocks future volume leverage.    │
│ • 2.3 Annual Estimated Volume: Shows total account lifetime value to the vendor.       │
│ • 2.4 MOQ Flexibility: Tells AI if buyer can take larger batches for tiered discounts. │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 💰 STAGE 3: COMMERCIAL & FINANCIAL BOUNDARIES                                          │
│ • 3.1 Initial Quote Price: Supplier's starting anchor point ($14.50).                  │
│ • 3.2 Target Price: Buyer's ideal price ($12.00) to calculate negotiation gap (17.2%). │
│ • 3.3 Walkaway Ceiling: Absolute maximum price ($13.50); AI ensures offers stay below. │
│ • 3.4 Preferred Payment Terms: Credit terms (Net 30/60) traded as chips for discounts. │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### 🎙️ Speaker Notes (What to say to Sir):
> *"Sir, this slide covers the first 3 stages of our intake questionnaire. Stage 1 captures technical specifications—ensuring we compare the exact metal grades and identifying if the part is an easy-to-replace commodity or locked-in custom tooling. Stage 2 captures volume scale, allowing buyers to leverage annual quantities for discounts. Stage 3 sets our financial boundaries: quote price, target price, and walkaway ceiling, which defines our 17% price gap."*
